# Photos of people we celebrate (Put Us On)

Only photos we have the right to use:

- **Permission:** they (or their team) sent it or said yes. Save it here, e.g. `amina.jpg`, and on the post set
  `"person_photo": "people/amina.jpg"` and `"photo_rights": "permission from Amina's manager, 12 Oct 2026"`.
- **Press kit:** an official press photo. Same as above, with `"photo_rights": "press kit: <link>"`.
- **Wikimedia Commons:** `"person_photo": "commons:File:<exact file name>"`. Accepted automatically only if the
  licence is CC0, public domain or CC BY, and credited on the design and in the caption.

Never: photos taken from their Instagram or Google Images without permission, stock lookalikes, or
AI-generated faces of real people. Tag them in the caption so they can share it.
